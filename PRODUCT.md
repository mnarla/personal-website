## Operating Context

Evaluated rapidly (30–60 seconds) by busy technical recruiters and engineers scanning for proof of work, architecture depth, live code links, and aesthetic taste. Must load instantly, feel mechanically responsive, and present dense technical telemetry without visual clutter.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** Pure semantic HTML5, modular Vanilla CSS architecture (`css/`), and modern native ES Modules (`js/`).
- **Performance:** Zero framework overhead, zero heavy bundle dependencies, instant first-contentful paint.
- **Interactivity:** Smoked obsidian glass telemetry HUD, real-time Pacific Time clock, scroll-linked progress beam, count-up tabular tickers, category filtering, and custom kinetic crosshair cursor.
- **Constraints:** Zero generic AI templates (no bulbous rounded-3xl cards, no purple-cyan gradient soup, no clichéd racing clip-art). 0px sharp-corner aesthetic.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Vibe:** Bold, motorsport-adjacent kinetic precision, high data density, and confident negative space.
- **Color Identity:** Deep textured charcoal canvas (`#121316`), dark graphite cards (`#18191E`), and high-voltage Electric Crimson (`#FF1E56`) accents limited to ~5% visual weight.
- **Typography:** Heavy condensed display headers (*Barlow Condensed*), clean human body copy (*Plus Jakarta Sans*), and tabular monospace telemetry labels (*JetBrains Mono*).
- **Voice:** Direct, human-voiced, punchy, and devoid of corporate fluff.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof of Work Over Resume Jargon:** Lead with architecture, live repos, and concrete technical metrics (e.g. 30+ FPS, 4-node graph, 0ms local lag).
2. **Kinetic Speed & Mechanical Easing:** Interfaces must feel tactile, snapping into state instantly rather than lagging behind soft floaty animations.
3. **Range First, Specialization Second:** Establish wide versatility across CV, distributed systems, and agentic AI before diving into domain-specific projects like motorsport telemetry.
4. **Extreme Craft & Zero AI Slop:** Every pixel, border, and interaction is intentional, sharp-cornered, and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Contrast ratio compliant (off-white `#F4F4F2` on charcoal `#121316` exceeds WCAG AAA standards).
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support disabling animations and scroll jumps.
- Accessible ARIA labels, semantic landmark roles (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`), and clean keyboard navigation.
